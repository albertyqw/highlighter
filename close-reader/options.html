Import your notes to Notion

Choose a destination once, then use Import to Notion in any page’s notes panel.

  1. Create an internal connection in Notion’s connection settings with Read content and Insert content capabilities. Copy its token.
  2. Open the Notion page you want to collect notes under. In its ••• → Connections menu, add that connection.
  3. Paste the token and destination page link below. Use a regular page as the destination.

The token is saved in this browser’s local extension storage, not synced. Local storage is not encrypted. Notes are sent directly to Notion only when you click Import. Each click creates a new page with the source link, grouped highlights, and notes.
