Import your notes to Reads

Connect your Reads database once, then choose topics and projects in any page’s notes panel.

  1. Create an internal connection in Notion’s connection settings with Read content and Insert content capabilities. Copy its token.
  2. Open your Reads database. In its ••• → Connections menu, add that connection.
  3. Paste the token below. Close Reader finds Reads automatically. You can paste the database link to choose a specific Reads database.

Reads needs a title property, a Date property, and multi-select properties named Topic Tags and Project. Existing choices are loaded from Notion; no tags are created.

The token is saved locally in this browser, not encrypted or synced. Opening the notes panel loads the available choices. Notes are sent directly to Notion when you click Import. Each import creates a new Reads page.
